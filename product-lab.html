Evidence practice

Specialist-led. Evidence-bound.

Product Lab is how Ingressible produces, organizes, and stands behind findings — testing, evidence, recommendations, and retesting, structured so a specialist's judgment stays at the center.

Capture → Organize → Review → Decide → Learn

Every engagement builds longitudinal evidence.

Structured, dated, and traceable — so learning compounds across engagements rather than evaporating after each report.

  1. Capture Observe and record the experience with the same rigor in every engagement.
  2. Organize Findings are classified, dated, and stored so they can be compared over time.
  3. Review The specialist weighs findings against the methods that produced them.
  4. Decide Recommendations follow from evidence. The specialist decides — AI assists, never decides.
  5. Learn Results are retested and accumulated — one authentic ground of future work.

Who holds the findings

The specialist observes. The specialist decides.

Research supports and AI assists — but neither replaces the specialist's judgment, and neither fabricates conclusions. Findings are only ever as strong as the method that produced them, and limitations are stated up front.

The evidence practice

What the Lab will never skip.

  • ✓
    Methods and limits, agreed first.

    Every assessment defines what will be measured, how, and what it cannot prove.

  • ✓
    Nothing fabricated, ever.

    No invented conclusions, benchmarks, thresholds, statistical significance, or causal claims.

  • ✓
    Confidential, by default.

    Client findings remain isolated and permission-aware. Nothing confidential is shared.

  • ✓
    Retested before it's real.

    Improvements are compared and retested against original findings before being called solved.
