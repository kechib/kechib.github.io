05 · Accessibility foundation

Designed in, not added later.

01

Operable

Keyboard-first controls, visible focus, generous touch targets, and no motion-dependent action.

02

Understandable

Semantic structure, plain labels, useful errors, and predictable state changes.

03

Adaptable

Reflow, zoom, reduced motion, forced colors, and robust screen-reader reading order.

04

Perceivable

AA contrast targets, text alternatives, captions, transcripts, and meaning beyond color.

WCAG 2.2 AA is the floor. The system also protects brand intention, user preference, and the agency to experience content in different ways.
