Consulting services

What Ingressible offers.

Six services, one thread: preserving what makes a beauty experience distinct while expanding who can meaningfully participate. Consulting is Ingressible's current business and stands independently of everything Ingressible builds next.

01 — ExperientialProduct & Packaging Accessibility

  • Opening
  • Closures
  • Grip & handling
  • Identification
  • Dispensing
  • Application
  • Instructions
  • Storage
  • Everyday use

Hands-on assessment of how a product behaves across the full lifecycle of use — from first touch to daily ritual. Findings are described precisely, honestly, and always tied to the specific product in front of us.

The work protects the sensory ritual that makes a beauty product delightful while making the routine — opening, using, storing — possible on your own terms.

02 — ExperientialRetail & Customer Experience

  • Discovery
  • Displays
  • Navigation
  • Testers & sampling
  • Consultation
  • Purchase
  • Pickup
  • Support
  • Omnichannel journeys

How an experience invites participation in person and online — and where the physical and digital journeys need to hold together as one, from the window display to the checkout and the follow-up.

Evaluations cover the entire journey with the customer's needs in mind, so that a beautiful store stays beautiful for people who navigate it differently.

03 — DigitalDigital Accessibility

  • Websites
  • Ecommerce
  • Mobile
  • Forms
  • Content
  • Customer support
  • Documents
  • Remediation guidance
  • Retesting

Accessibility testing, findings, and practical remediation guidance for websites, ecommerce, mobile experiences, forms, and documents.

Assessment methods and any supported standards are documented in advance, and improvements are retested so the evidence stays current.

04 — IterativePrototype Development & Testing

  • Concept review
  • Hands-on assessment
  • Design alternatives
  • Collaboration
  • Version comparisons
  • Retesting

Working alongside your team on low-fidelity and functional prototypes, assessing alternatives hands-on, and retesting improvements against the original findings.

Ideas are compared honestly against evidence rather than intuition — so good design survives contact with real use.

05 — AdvisoryTraining & Advisory

  • Role-specific training
  • Demonstrations
  • Document guidance
  • Working sessions
  • Office hours
  • Ongoing support

Training and advisory that fits real roles — not generic decks. Accessible design, inclusive language, packaging guidance, and supported practice.

The goal is team capability rather than dependency: your people learn to see, question, and improve on their own.

06 — ResearchResearch & Inclusive Innovation

  • Research reviews
  • Opportunity mapping
  • Question development
  • Participant-informed work

Research reviews and opportunity mapping, plus participant-informed work when explicitly scoped. Ingressible does not claim that simulations reproduce lived disability — and work involving people is always scoped honestly.

Every finding and benchmark is traceable to the method that produced it, and nothing is claimed beyond what the evidence supports.

How engagements work

Flexible by design, honest by default.

Assessments

A defined scope, clear methods, findings that stand independently, and recommendations that respect your brand's intention.

Training & advisory

Working sessions, office hours, and guidance that build your team's capability rather than dependency.

Prototyping & retesting

Iterative, hands-on work where improvements are tried, compared, and retested against original findings.

Professional boundaries

Assessments use professional testing, measurement, and simulation methods. Ingressible does not position digital certifications or simulations as proof of expertise in physical product design, and does not publish confidential client findings. Illustrative samples are always labeled as such.

Getting started

Start with a conversation. Ingressible will scope the work, define methods and limitations up front, and only report what evidence can support.
