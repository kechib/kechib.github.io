Method, first

Simple on the surface. Expert underneath.

Every engagement follows a defined cycle — discover, question, specify, plan, approve, build, test, refine. Methods and limitations are set in advance, so evidence drives every decision a specialist makes.

The working cycle

Capture → Organize → Review → Decide.

Learn → Evaluate → Improve. Each engagement moves deliberately through eight steps — and the cycle loops whenever improvements need retesting.

Discover

Understand the product, packaging, space, or screen — and the intention behind it.

Question

Ask what participation should look like, and what the experience must preserve.

Specify

Define the work, the methods, and the success criteria before anything is measured.

Plan

Lay out the assessment, its timeline, and its limits — agreed in advance.

Approve

The scope is reviewed and approved before evidence work begins.

Build

Conduct the assessment, and evaluate the experience as it actually is.

Test

Gather findings and retest improvements against original evidence.

Refine

Iterate, compare, and update findings — then begin the cycle again.

Who decides

Taste, judgment, and evidence stay human.

The specialist observes. The Workspace organizes. Research supports. AI assists. The specialist decides.

Distributive design

Beauty first, then accessibility.

Ingressible's balance is not a compromise — it is a sequence and a commitment. Around 70%: beauty, editorial expression, and the human experience. Around 30%: technology, accessibility, research, and inclusive innovation.

The 70% protects what makes a brand itself. The 30% ensures who can enter it. Neither is optional.

Expert underneath

The interface gets quieter as the work gets more important. Behind the calm exterior sits professional testing, documented methods, and specialized knowledge earned through certification and practice.

Simple on the surface

Clients experience a clear conversation, a clean page, and decisions they can follow. The complexity is Ingressible's to manage — not the client's to decode.

Finding language

Every finding is named, evidenced, and scoped.

Findings are framed in a shared language so they are comparable, traceable, and honest. Illustrative examples below.

S

Strength

Something the experience already does well — worth preserving or extending.

Observed: unambiguous, confirmed in testing
B

Barrier

Something that prevents or hinders meaningful participation.

Observed: evidenced against the method; severity graded
O

Opportunity

A design direction that could expand participation without losing the brand.

Proposed: recommendation, not yet proven

Working agreements

What you can rely on.

  • ✓
    Methods and limits, set in advance.

    What will be measured, how, and what it can't prove — agreed before evidence work starts.

  • ✓
    Evidence over assertion.

    Findings are traceable to the method that produced them. No fabricated conclusions, thresholds, or significance.

  • ✓
    Confidentiality by design.

    Client findings stay isolated and permission-aware. Nothing confidential is published.

  • ✓
    Improvements are retested.

    Recommendations aren't final words — they are tested again against the original findings.
